Full GRPO run: reinforcement learning for operative notes

Results of the full reinforcement-learning run on PitVis-2023: a Qwen3.5-9B note writer trained with GRPO and verifiable, label-based rewards, evaluated on five held-out procedures against the perception-only template and the untrained model.

1 · Headline

The trained note writer outperforms both baselines

On all 85 held-out prompts, the GRPO model scores 2.364 on the combined reward, against 2.244 for the template and 2.238 for the untrained model. It leads on grounding, instruments, timing, calibration and conciseness, matches the template on step F1, and every note it writes is complete and well-formed.

2 · Experiment

What was trained and how

Video, 1 fps→ Endo-FM (frozen)→ MS-TCN steps + instruments→ Facts (segments, confidence, reliability)→ Qwen3.5-9B + LoRA, trained with GRPO→ Structured note with [verify] flags
SettingValue
Data split17 PitVis videos for GRPO (258 prompts: full procedures + 15-minute windows) · videos 5, 12, 18 for checkpoint selection (44 prompts) · videos 21–25 held out (85 prompts)
PromptsBuilt from out-of-fold perception output for training videos; rewards computed against the expert labels
Rewards (weight)Grounding (1.0) · calibration (0.5) · timing (0.3) · safety (0.3) · format (0.2) · concise (0.5) — maximum 2.8
Optimisation400 steps · 8 notes per prompt · learning rate 5e-6 with cosine decay · KL coefficient 0.03 · gradient clipping 0.5 · length-unbiased dr_grpo loss · 900-token completion budget
SafeguardsFull checkpoints every 25 steps, all kept and synced to S3 · training watchdog on KL, gradient norm, clipping, length and reward · checkpoint chosen on validation videos
ComputeOne NVIDIA L40S (48 GB), about 50 s per step, 5 h 36 min of training

Changes since the smoke test: a conciseness reward with a length budget, partial credit for truncated notes, a prompt rule to merge interrupted steps, stronger KL regularisation with a decaying learning rate, and validation-based checkpoint selection.

  • Validation improved at every checkpoint, from 2.193 (step 50) to 2.308 (step 400), with most of the gain by step 200. Step 400 was selected.
  • Training stayed stable: KL to the starting model stayed below 0.025, gradient norms below 0.5 on average, note length between 300 and 390 tokens, and no completion reached the length budget. The watchdog never intervened.

The full-run model recovers three steps the pilot left out (septum displacement, sellotomy and durotomy), keeping the two uncertain ones flagged for the surgeon rather than omitting them, and merges five fragmented tumour-excision lines into one continuous phase. This is the behaviour the reliability-weighted recall reward and the conciseness reward are designed to produce.
